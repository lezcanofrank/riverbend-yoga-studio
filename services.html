Classes and Services

Riverbend offers several ways to practice, from gentle group classes to focused private sessions. Students are encouraged to choose the pace and level of support that best fit their needs.

Group Classes

Gentle Yoga

Slow movements, supported poses, and clear instruction make this class a comfortable choice for beginners and students who prefer a lower-impact practice.

Vinyasa Flow

A moderately paced class connects movement with breathing. Instructors provide options so students can adjust the intensity while developing strength and coordination.

Restorative Yoga

Longer, supported poses encourage relaxation and recovery. This quiet class is useful for releasing tension after a busy day or balancing more active exercise.

Private Sessions

One-on-one sessions give students personalized guidance based on their goals, experience, mobility, and comfort level. Private instruction may be useful for learning fundamentals, adapting poses, or creating an individual practice plan.

Pricing Overview

  • Single-class passes are available for occasional visits.
  • Multi-class packages offer flexibility for regular students.
  • Monthly memberships support a consistent practice.
  • Private sessions are priced separately based on session length.
