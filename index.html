Welcome to Riverbend Yoga Studio

A Supportive Place to Practice

Riverbend Yoga Studio offers calm, welcoming classes for people who want to improve strength, flexibility, balance, and everyday well-being. Our mission is to make yoga approachable for beginners, busy professionals, and older adults in a respectful community setting.

New to Yoga

No previous experience or special flexibility is required. Instructors explain each pose, offer modifications, and encourage students to rest whenever needed. Comfortable clothing and a willingness to learn are enough for a first class.

Featured Class

Gentle Foundations

This beginner-friendly class introduces basic poses, breathing techniques, and safe transitions at an unhurried pace. It is a helpful starting point for anyone returning to movement or building confidence before joining other classes.

Weekly Schedule Overview

  • Morning: Gentle Yoga and Vinyasa Flow
  • Midday: Chair Yoga and Private Sessions
  • Evening: Restorative Yoga and Beginner Foundations
  • Weekends: Community classes and monthly workshops
